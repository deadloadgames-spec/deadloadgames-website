02Description

Load Path is a cozy office builder and automation game where, after your mentor retires, you're put in charge of running your very own engineering firm. You'll design the office, set up the flow of paperwork, and keep your engineers happy (or not) as your firm grows from a single room into a multi-storey high-rise megacorp of engineering mastery.

If you've ever wondered what an engineer does, look no further. This game was created by a structural engineer who craves efficiency and wanted to see just how streamlined a real engineering firm could become.

Build your office

Lay out your office in whatever way you think best. Floor-by-floor, you'll be able to build your office endlessly upwards, with more space for more departments, to generate more income.

Automate the design process

Just like a real engineering firm, every paycheque starts with a client meeting. Of course, your time is too valuable to attend every meeting yourself, so you'll need to hire engineers to do it for you.

Engineers won't just show up and start working, you'll need to give them breakrooms, IT support, training and pep talks to keep the money flowing. From humble beginnings doing contract work, to designing retaining walls, and then eventually onto giant high-rise buildings, you'll do it all...well, you'll get your staff to do it all.

Engineering minigames

Clients don't always like what you deliver to them, and unfortunately, you're the one who has to solve their problems. They'll send projects and submissions back to you, requiring you to check designs, touch up drawings, re-align roads and more!

Manage your people

Managing people isn't an engineer's strongest skill, but you'll have to hope luck is on your side and give it a go anyway! You'll be able to sit down with individual staff for one-on-one pep talks or, if you don't have the time, you can call a big staff meeting to try and encourage everyone together.

Grow the firm

Clients won't just hand their hardest projects to a group of rag-tag engineers with no prestige. You'll need to build your reputation and prove you are capable by delivering projects, managing your employees, and dealing with your client's problems.

Welcome to Load Path. Where all the stress leads to you.
